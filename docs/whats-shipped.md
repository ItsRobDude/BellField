| Invoice payments (M8/6b)       | Payments are job-level append-only ledger rows with allocations to posted charge invoices. `payments.invoice_id` is the initiating/source invoice context, not a guarantee that allocation landed on that same invoice; allocation remains job-level/main-first until a later explicit source-invoice-first slice. The live Stripe sandbox refund smoke passed on 2026-06-15 Pacific / 2026-06-16 UTC. Amount-scoped Stripe Checkout payment links now exist for posted invoices: office chooses an amount up to the current amount due and creates/copies the link, relay creates the connected-account Checkout Session with BellField's platform fee, Stripe webhook confirmation lands on the relay, and the worker polls/acks events before recording the local payment idempotently by provider payment id. Job-level deposit links now exist from the draft invoice surface: paid deposits record as unallocated job credit, immediately reduce job amount due, and stay visible in the payment ledger. A durable `payment.purpose` (`payment`/`deposit`) is carried end to end — set at link creation, stamped by the worker from the session, and recorded directly for manual deposits — so the ledger shows `Deposit received · <method>` vs `Payment received · <method>` from real data (never inferred from a null invoice), and the payment-ledger CSV carries a `Purpose` column. The live Stripe sandbox payment-link depth smoke passed on 2026-06-15 Pacific / 2026-06-16 UTC. Amount due is derived from posted main + adjustments − credits − non-void payments + refunds. Money-safety hardening (PR #18/#19 follow-up): deterministic per-(job,source,amount,attempt) link idempotency; active unpaid links are reused locally; paid same-dollar repeats require office confirmation before a fresh attempt; invoice links that could exceed current amount due while other unpaid links are still active require office confirmation; card-only v1; relay-owned customer redirect URLs; webhook reconciliation against the stored session (amount/currency/connected-account) with zero-amount and duplicate-event no-ops; and confirmed payments are recorded in full, with overpayments or local link mismatches surfaced on the timeline for review instead of poisoning relay retries. Customer card surcharge / processing-fee pass-through is intentionally not planned for v1 unless real customer demand justifies a dedicated legal and card-network review. Company settings now include invoice email subject/body defaults. An optional owner setting (`includeInvoicePaymentLink`, default off) embeds this pay-now link in invoice emails for posted **main** invoices with an outstanding balance; the link is minted only after the send is reserved and the PDF renders, and a link failure never blocks the invoice send. **Customer payment receipt emails (slice 1a)** now send automatically when a manual cash/check/card payment or deposit is recorded: the receipt is enqueued in the same money transaction (exactly-once, unique per payment), and a worker send-loop resolves the bill-to customer's primary email, renders the body from owner-editable settings (`sendPaymentReceipts` toggle default on, plus subject/body templates with `{customerName}/{amount}/{method}/{date}/{jobNumber}/{receiptKind}` tokens), pins recipient/copy on the first attempt so retries never drift, and sends through the relay's billing sender on a **separate transactional path** (no PDF, no acceptance) — deliberately not the PDF-shaped estimate/invoice document outbox. A customer with no email on file is logged office-only and never blocks the payment. **Online card payment receipts (slice 1b)** now send too: the worker enqueues the receipt in the same transaction that records a provider-confirmed online payment/deposit, only on first apply (`'applied'`), so an at-least-once relay redelivery never double-receipts and previously-applied payments are never retroactively receipted; the receipt amount/purpose mirror the recorded payment, and `occurred_at` is Stripe's paid time. Refund receipts (manual + online) remain a follow-on slice. Note: BellField now owns the payment receipt; if a connected account also has Stripe's automatic customer receipt emails enabled, the customer could receive both (go-live checklist item).
