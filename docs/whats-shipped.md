A job invoice balance (`GET .../invoice/balance`) reports net billed plus paid/amount-due. Office UI: the Invoice tab shows the balance and lets bookkeeping add/post adjustments and credits.                              | `apps/api/src/modules/invoices`, `apps/api/src/database/migrations/20260601_008_invoice_adjustments.up.sql`, `apps/office-web/src/modules/operations/job-invoice-corrections.tsx`                                                                                                                                                        |
