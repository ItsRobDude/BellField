Amount-scoped Stripe Checkout payment links now exist for posted invoices: office chooses an amount up to the current amount due and creates/copies the link, relay creates the connected-account Checkout Session with BellField's fixed 100 bps / 1% platform fee, Stripe webhook confirmation lands on the relay, and the worker polls/acks events before recording the local payment idempotently by provider payment id. Job-level deposit links now exist from the draft invoice surface: paid deposits record as null-source job-level payments; they stay unallocated credit when no posted charges exist, allocate job-wide/main-first when posted charges already exist, and existing pre-post deposit credit allocates to the main invoice when that main invoice posts. The office can also **Record a manual job-level deposit** (cash/check/card it took directly) from the same surface (`POST /operations/jobs/:jobId/payments/deposit`, `payments:create`). A durable `payment.purpose` (`payment`/`deposit`) is carried end to end — set at link creation, stamped by the worker from the session, and recorded directly for manual deposits — so the ledger shows `Deposit received · <method>` vs `Payment received · <method>` from real data (never inferred from a null invoice), and the payment-ledger CSV carries a `Purpose` column. The live Stripe sandbox payment-link depth smoke passed on 2026-06-15 Pacific / 2026-06-16 UTC. Amount due is derived from posted main + adjustments − credits − non-void payments + refunds. Money-safety hardening (PR #18/#19 follow-up): deterministic per-(job,source,amount,attempt) link idempotency; active unpaid links are reused locally; paid same-dollar repeats require office confirmation before a fresh attempt; invoice links that could exceed current amount due while other unpaid links are still active require office confirmation; card-only v1; relay-owned customer redirect URLs; webhook reconciliation against the stored session (amount/currency/connected-account) with zero-amount and duplicate-event no-ops; and confirmed payments are recorded in full, with overpayments or local link mismatches surfaced on the timeline for review instead of poisoning relay retries. Customer card surcharge / processing-fee pass-through is intentionally not planned for v1 unless real customer demand justifies a dedicated legal and card-network review.
